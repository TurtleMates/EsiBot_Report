\label{sec:resultats_derived}

The operating parameters declared in the architecture dictate the robot's overall
behavior. Table~\ref{tab:derived_values} gathers the key characteristics derived from the
system configuration.

\begin{table}[H]
        \centering
        \caption{Quantities derived from the configuration parameters.}
        \label{tab:derived_values}
        \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{L{6.4cm} C{2.6cm} L{3.5cm}}
                \toprule
                \textbf{Quantity} & \textbf{Value} & \textbf{Calculation / source} \\
                \midrule
                Effective encoder resolution & 24 pulses/rev & \texttt{encoder\_cpr}=12, dual-edge counting \\
                Control loop / \gls{imu} / \gls{ekf} frequencies & 10 / 50 / 30\,Hz & \texttt{control\_hz}, \texttt{mpu6050\_node}, \texttt{ekf\_odom\_imu.yaml} \\

                Raw \topic{/scan} rate / \gls{slam}-integrated rate & $\approx$30\,Hz / $\leq$2\,Hz & \texttt{scan\_time}=0.033\,s; interval=0.5\,s \\

                \bottomrule
        \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{notebox}[title={Velocity Asymmetry}]
        The maximum angular velocity in manual teleoperation (2.4\,rad/s) is set higher
        than the autonomous speed to ensure strong human responsiveness.
\end{notebox}
